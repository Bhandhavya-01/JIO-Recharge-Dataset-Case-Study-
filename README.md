# JIO-Recharge-Dataset-Case-Study

📊 JIO Recharge Dataset – Trend Forecasting

JIO Recharge Dataset case study analyzes recharge patterns to forecast demand, optimize network resources, and enhance customer retention. It covers data cleaning, trend analysis, forecasting by plan/city, and key insights. Recommendations include targeted promotions, network upgrades, and payment incentives for growth.
JIO REcharge Dataset Case study.

🚀 Project Overview
This case study explores recharge patterns of Reliance JIO, one of India’s largest telecom operators, to analyze customer behavior and forecast future demand.
By studying recharge trends (amounts, plans, cities, and payment modes), JIO can optimize network resources, design smarter offers, and improve customer satisfaction.

🎯 Objectives

🔍 Analyze recharge data to identify usage patterns.

📈 Forecast future demand using time-series models.

🌐 Understand regional trends (city-wise recharge activity).

💡 Detect customer preferences (plans, payment modes).

🎁 Provide business recommendations for marketing, promotions, and infrastructure planning.

🛠️ Methodology

Data Cleaning & Preparation

Handling missing values, duplicates, and outliers.

Formatting dates & extracting features (day, week, month).

Trend Analysis

Recharge volumes (daily, weekly, monthly).

Prepaid vs Postpaid analysis.

Popular plans & payment modes.

City-wise recharge frequency.

Forecasting

Applied Moving Average, Exponential Smoothing, and ARIMA models.

Forecasted recharge demand for next 1–3 months by plan type and city.

Key Insights

📊 Peak recharge during festivals, weekends, and salary days.

💳 UPI & Wallets are dominating payment methods.

🌆 Tier-2 cities like Lucknow & Indore show fast growth.

📱 ₹399 & ₹599 combo plans remain most popular.

📌 Business Recommendations

🎯 Targeted promotions during low-demand periods.

📶 Network upgrades in high-growth cities.

💰 Incentivize UPI/wallet payments to cut transaction costs.

📦 Design new combo plans based on trending recharge amounts.

🏆 Conclusion

The project demonstrates how data-driven insights can help JIO:
✔ Optimize resources & network planning.
✔ Personalize offers for better engagement.
✔ Predict future demand for strategic growth.
✔ Enhance customer satisfaction & loyalty.
